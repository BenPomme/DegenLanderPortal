HOW TO PLAY

  • Press Space or click SPIN to start the reels. Every reel then scrolls until you stop it.
  • Press Space again to stop them one at a time, left to right.
  • Reel 1 is free choice: stop it on the biggest symbol you can. Reels 2 and 3 then show an arrow where your target symbol is, and you have to hit it twice.
  • Stopping dead centre is a perfect stop. All three perfect adds 50% to a three-of-a-kind payout.
  • Raise the bet for bigger swings. It is deducted nowhere but the payouts scale with it.
Not financial advice. Every spin costs you the stake. These reels are the one thing in this arcade you actually control.
