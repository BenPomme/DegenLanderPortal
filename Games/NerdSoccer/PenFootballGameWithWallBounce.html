HOW IT LEARNS

Every shot you take is a training example. A small neural network learns to map your pose before contact onto the part of the goal you actually hit, and two Q-learners work out which saves and which shots pay off by playing. The weights are saved in your browser, so it is still better next time.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Left and right to run. Build up speed before you hit the ball, because contact power scales with how fast you are moving into it.
  • Up or Space to jump, twice for a double jump. Hitting the ball downward off a jump is the strongest strike.
  • Strike the ball off-centre to put spin on it. Curl is drawn as a ring around the ball: cyan one way, purple the other.
  • The gold band on your goal is where the bot currently thinks you are going to shoot.
  • First to five. It remembers you between matches.
